Privacy Policy for GracelandMiles

Last updated: September 26, 2026

GracelandMiles ("the app") is developed by Randy Adams ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Location data

The app uses your device's location, with your permission, solely to calculate the distance and direction from your current position to Graceland and other Elvis-related sites shown in the app. This location data is used only on your device to perform that calculation. It is not stored by us, transmitted to our servers, or shared with any third party. We do not operate a server that receives your location.

Advertising

The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID) and other technical information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control what AdMob collects; you can review or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Purchases

The optional "Pro" upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — that information is handled directly by Google, per Google's Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase is active, to unlock Pro features.

Accounts

The app does not require or support creating an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers.

Children

GracelandMiles is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to randy@adamselite.com.
